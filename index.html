Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you might want to know before reaching out — services, pricing, timelines, tech stack and how to start a project.

What services do you offer?

WordPress & WooCommerce development (custom themes, Elementor/Bricks builds, headless with WPGraphQL), Shopify (Liquid themes, Hydrogen headless), custom React/Next.js web apps and dashboards, plus SEO and ongoing maintenance. I focus on building fast, modern sites that convert — not just look pretty.

How much does a typical website cost?

Projects typically range from $300 for a small landing page to $3,000+ for a full WooCommerce or Shopify store with custom integrations. Each quote is custom because no two scopes are the same. Send the brief through the contact form on the main portfolio and you'll get an itemized quote within 24 hours.

How long does a project usually take?

A small business site takes 1–2 weeks, a WooCommerce or Shopify store 3–5 weeks, and a custom React/SaaS dashboard 4–8 weeks. Rush delivery is available — I've shipped landing pages in 48 hours when needed. Timeline depends on how ready your content, brand assets and product data are on day one.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance after launch?

Yes. I offer monthly retainers covering plugin/theme updates, daily backups, security monitoring, uptime checks and small content changes. Retainers start at $60/month. One-off fixes, feature additions and emergency support are also available — just message me.

Can you redesign or fix an existing website?

Absolutely — this is actually a big chunk of my work. I routinely take over existing WordPress, WooCommerce and Shopify sites, whether for a full redesign, performance optimization, recovering a hacked site, or fixing a previous developer's work. Send me the URL and what's broken or feels dated and I'll send a recovery/redesign plan.

What technologies do you specialize in?

WordPress + Elementor/Bricks/ACF, WooCommerce (custom product types, subscriptions, B2B catalogs), Shopify (Liquid + Hydrogen + custom apps), React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and headless WordPress with WPGraphQL. For SEO and analytics: RankMath/Yoast, Schema.org structured data, GA4, GTM, and Core Web Vitals optimization.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I'm based in Pakistan and work with clients across the UK, US, UAE, Australia and Europe. All communication is in English, I overlap with most time zones (typically online 9am–11pm PKT), and I accept payment via Wise, PayPal, bank transfer and Stripe.

Do you offer SEO services?

Every build ships with on-page SEO best practices baked in — semantic HTML, fast Core Web Vitals, image optimization, schema.org structured data, sitemaps and clean meta tags. For ongoing SEO campaigns (keyword research, content briefs, technical audits, link-building outreach), I offer monthly packages starting at $200/month.

How do we get started?

Head to furqan-ghias.vercel.app/#contact, fill out the brief (project type, scope, timeline, budget range), and I'll respond within 24 hours with a quote, a rough timeline and a discovery-call link if needed. The 50% deposit secures your slot — work typically starts within a week.

What's your payment structure?

Typically 50% upfront to start, 50% on delivery. For larger projects (>$2,000), milestone-based payments are available — e.g. 25% / 25% / 25% / 25% across design, build, revisions, launch. Payment via Wise, PayPal, bank transfer or Stripe. Invoices provided for every transaction.

Still have a question? I usually reply within 24 hours.
